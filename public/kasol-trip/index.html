The big picture

Two ways to finish the same trip

Everyone travels together from Fri 9 Oct to Sat 17 Oct. From Sunday 18 Oct the group splits by choice: Track A flies home from Delhi that evening, while Track B stays on for two easy Delhi days and flies home on Tue 20 Oct. The mountain part has no rushed transfers: one trek day, one long drive per travel day, and a rest day after every big effort.

Bangalore✈Delhi→Kasol→Kheerganga→Kasol→Manali→Sissu→Delhi✈Bangalore

All drive times and prices are estimates. Confirm road, trail and bus details before booking.

Day by day

Your relaxed itinerary

FRI · OCT 09Day 0

Bangalore → Delhi ✈

  • Take an evening direct flight (about 3 hours), landing between 8 pm and 11 pm.
  • Take a cab or metro to a hotel near the airport (Aerocity or Mahipalpur). Eat, sleep, no rush.
Leave dayStay: Delhi
SAT · OCT 10Day 1 · Weekend

Slow Delhi day → overnight bus

  • Sleep in, late check-out, then a light outing such as Humayun's Tomb or Lodhi Garden.
  • Have dinner by 6 pm and reach the bus pick-up point 45 minutes early.
  • Board the Volvo to Bhuntar/Kasol at 6–8 pm. Book all seats together and carry a neck pillow and a layer.
Overnight bus
SUN · OCT 11Day 2 · Weekend

Arrive Kasol, settle in

  • The bus reaches Bhuntar in the morning. Take a pre-booked cab for the last 1.5 hours up the Parvati Valley.
  • Check in, nap, then stroll the riverside and Kasol cafés. Nothing else is planned.
Stay: KasolEasy day
MON · OCT 12Day 3

Manikaran + Chalal

  • Cab to Manikaran Sahib (about 30 minutes) for the gurdwara, langar and hot springs.
  • After lunch, walk the forest trail to Chalal, an easy hour or so each way.
  • Early night, because tomorrow is the trek.
Leave dayStay: Kasol
TUE · OCT 13Day 4

Kheerganga trek · ascent

  • Cab to Barshaini by 8 am and start walking by 9 am with a local guide.
  • Allow 5–7 hours uphill with rest stops. Carry only a small daypack, and leave big bags at your Kasol hotel.
  • Stay overnight at a camp or guesthouse only if permitted. Confirm before you go.
Leave dayTrek day 1Stay: Kheerganga*

Sunrise → descend → rest

Leave dayStay: Kasol
THU · OCT 15Day 6

Kasol → Manali

  • Leave after breakfast, at about 10 am. The drive via Bhuntar and Kullu takes 4–5 hours with stops.

Flight details

Flights for 8–9 people

Outbound · Fri 9 Oct

  • Route: Bangalore (BLR) to Delhi (DEL), direct, about 2 hr 45 min to 3 hr 15 min.
  • Target departure: 4 pm to 8 pm, so you land before midnight.
  • Typical direct carriers: IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air.

Return · Track A Sun 18 / Track B Tue 20

  • Track A: DEL to BLR after 6 pm on Sun 18 Oct.
  • Track B: any convenient flight on Tue 20 Oct.
  • Both tracks can book the same outbound flight and split only on the return.
Booking tips

The trip is only a few weeks away, so book soon. Book the outbound together for all 8–9 people so you sit together. Book returns separately for each track. Check the Delhi terminal for your airline and baggage allowance. Fares and schedules here are not live, so verify them on the airline or booking site before paying.

Getting around

Transport plan

Option A · Buses + local cabs

The budget option. Overnight Volvos on Sat 10 and Sat 17, and local cabs in the valley.

  • Book Volvo seats together, at least 2 weeks ahead.
  • Pre-book a 9-seater or two cabs from Bhuntar to Kasol.
  • Keep luggage light, since bus storage is limited.

Option B · Private tempo traveller

One 12-seater for the whole circuit. More flexible, and often costlier.

  • Get written quotes with tolls, parking, driver allowance and night charges.
  • Confirm luggage space and mountain-road experience.
  • Compare against the bus plus cabs total.

Where to stay

Stays for a group of 8–9

Delhi · Fri 9 Oct (1 night)

Near the airport for a smooth arrival: Aerocity hotels (₹4,000–7,000 per room) or budget Mahipalpur hotels (₹2,000–3,500). Ask for a late check-out.

Kasol · 3 nights

Riverside hotels, cottages or a group hostel dorm. Expect ₹1,500–3,500 per room or ₹700–1,200 per bed. Ask for 4 rooms, or a cottage with a shared sitting area. Leave big bags here during the trek.

Kheerganga · 1 night (if allowed)

Tents or basic guesthouses at ₹500–1,200 per person. These are simple, so carry a warm layer, headlamp and sleeping bag if needed.

Manali · 2 nights

Old Manali for cafés and a village feel, or Mall Road for easy transport. Expect ₹2,000–4,500 per room. Choose a place with a group-friendly lobby and hot water.

Delhi · Track A day room

A day-use room (₹1,500–3,000) on Sun 18 Oct lets everyone shower before the flight.

Delhi · Track B, 2 nights

Central Delhi (Connaught Place, Karol Bagh, or near a metro line) at ₹2,500–5,000 per room. Book from Sun 18 Oct so you can check in when the bus arrives.

Room rates are rough estimates for mid-range stays and vary with demand. Confirm room occupancy and cancellation terms before you pay.

Eat well

Kasol food and café trail

Kasol is known for its riverside cafés and Israeli-style menus, and the rest of the route has great local food too. Here is what to eat and when, matched to your itinerary. Expect about ₹300–700 per person per meal at cafés.

Must-try list

  • Israeli breakfast plate and shakshuka in Kasol
  • Siddu and Himachali dham thali
  • Trout in Manali
  • Plain chocolate brownies, apple pie and hot chocolate
  • Seabuckthorn tea and local apple products

Café tips

  • Pick busy cafés with recent reviews, since places open and close often.
  • Ask for a group table at 6 pm before the evening rush.
  • Cafés that say "special" on the menu are not for us. Order plain items only.
  • Carry cash, as UPI signal is patchy in the valley.

Stay healthy

  • Drink bottled or filtered water only.
  • Eat hot, freshly cooked food and avoid salads from unknown stalls.
  • Carry ORS, antacids and basic stomach medicine.
  • Eat light the night before the trek.

Vegetarian and special diets

Kasol and Manali are easy for vegetarians, with plenty of paneer, pasta, pizza and falafel options. Tell your cafe about allergies when ordering, and check menus for nuts and dairy.
