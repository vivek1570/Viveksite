The big picture

A loop that keeps travel sensible

Use Delhi as your flight gateway, travel north to the Parvati Valley, continue to Manali and Lahaul, then return to Delhi for your flight home. October brings autumn mountain scenery, but fresh snow around Kasol/Manali is not guaranteed.

Bangalore✈Delhi→Kasol→Kheerganga→Tosh→Manali→Sissu→Delhi✈Bangalore

*Includes two overnight bus journeys; actual nights depend on flight and bus timings. Treat all drive times as estimates.

Getting around

Transport plan for 8–9 people

Option A · Buses + local cabs

Fly BLR–DEL, take an overnight bus to Kasol, use local cabs for valley transfers, then bus Manali–Delhi. Usually the more budget-conscious structure.

  • Book bus seats together early.
  • Allow 12–15 hours for Delhi–Kasol/Manali–Delhi.
  • Keep the Delhi arrival day light.

Option B · Private 12-seater tempo traveller

Get a quote for the Himachal circuit, with luggage capacity confirmed. More flexible stops and one group vehicle, but potentially costlier.

  • Ask for tolls, parking, driver allowance, taxes and night charges in writing.
  • Confirm vehicle suitability for mountain roads.
  • Compare total quote against bus + local taxi costs.

Riverside hotel, cottages or group hostel. Ask for 4 rooms or a private cottage with a shared sitting area.

Mountain-view guesthouse for a village experience. Skip the overnight if you prefer less packing and more convenient transport.

Group-friendly hotel with easy access to Solang and the Atal Tunnel route.

Delhi · 1 night

Central Delhi for sightseeing or Aerocity for airport convenience. Overnight buses account for two travel nights.

Before you go

Booking checklist

Plan with flexibility

October is autumn in Himachal, not a dependable snowfall window. Treat Sissu as a high-altitude scenery excursion, not a snow guarantee. Avoid adding Shimla, Dharamshala or Jibhi to this loop—the extra transfers can make the trip feel rushed.

Verify current trail permissions, road conditions, bus schedules, attraction opening days and flight fares before booking. Festival dates and local demand may affect availability.
